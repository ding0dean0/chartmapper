## 🔑 Key Insights
1. **Commercial peak, cultural moment.** Two consecutive UK #1s with 43.7% sales growth between albums. Peters is in an ascent phase — format and catalogue decisions made now will benefit from significant tailwind.
2. **The Taylor Swift proximity is a double-edged asset.** The Eras Tour association brings scale and credibility but also risks reducing perceived originality. The critical framing alongside Rodrigo and Chappell Roan (rather than Swift-adjacent) is the healthier long-term narrative.
3. **Physical buyer base is real and activated.** Topping the UK vinyl chart week-1 with 8,935 units is a committed fanbase spending money — high-conversion audience for limited editions and back-catalogue physical releases.

- **15-track sequencing** — streaming drop-off after track 8–10 is a real risk; worth monitoring Spotify skip rates and playlist add rates for second-half tracks
- **Reddit absence** — lack of indexable fan discussion threads is a mild signal; a fragmented community is harder to activate for release moments
